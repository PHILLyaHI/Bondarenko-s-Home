# Bondarenko Home Photography

## Register

brand

## Product purpose

Marketing site for Ben Bondarenko, a Seattle real estate photographer. Converts listing agents and homeowners who land on the site (Instagram link, text from a friend, Google search) into booked shoots. The site IS the brand — design quality is the differentiator.

## Users

- **Listing agents** at Compass, Windermere, John L. Scott, Redfin, etc. Hunting for a photographer who'll make their listing the one buyers click first. Read on phone between showings.
- **Top-producing realtors** with multi-million-dollar listings who need premium-feeling marketing. Pay attention to design quality as a proxy for craft.
- **Homeowners** selling FSBO or shopping for a photographer their agent recommended. Want their home to look like architecture-magazine spreads.

## Promise

Make your listing the one buyers click first. Cinematic, twilight-signature real estate photography across the Puget Sound. Eight years, 1,200+ listings, 32% faster average sales.

## Brand

- **Tone**: confident, terse, editorial. Closer to a film cinematographer's reel than a photographer's portfolio. Mono-uppercase metadata as design language (EXIF readouts on every card). Two-tone editorial headlines. Italics for emphasis.
- **Voice**: short sentences. "Make your listing the one buyers click first." "His signature, the moment the lights come on." Never marketing-speak.
- **Anti-references**: SmugMug template portfolios. Wedding-photographer pastel scripts. Stock-photo agent headshots. Any site that screams "Squarespace template."
- **Aesthetic family**: editorial / cinematic / architectural-digest. Photo-forward. Not minimalist-tech, not maximalist-art. Restrained type, dramatic photography.

## Strategic principles

- **Mobile-first, always.** Every layout, type ramp, tap target sized for a phone screen first.
- **Photos are the heart.** Type and chrome stay quiet so the architectural images dominate every screen.
- **EXIF as language.** Mono-uppercase metadata appears on every section. Positions Ben as a director of architectural light, not a button-pusher.
- **Twilight is the signature.** The portfolio's twilight shots are Ben's competitive advantage; the site visually leans into dusk, golden hour, blue hour as a narrative arc.
- **One conversion goal.** Every section ends in a "Book a shoot" path. Sticky bottom CTA on mobile. No newsletter, no blog, no distractions.
